Who walked it

Three PV roles, one hand-off chain

CP

Case processor

Intake and triage

Get every incoming report to a valid / not-valid decision inside the 7- and 15-day reporting clocks.

  • Needs the serious cases first
  • Works fast and scans lists
  • Must be able to work when the AI is down
SP

Safety physician

Signal assessment

Decide whether a drug–event pair is a real signal, with causality they can defend in front of a committee.

  • Wants evidence, not explanations of the tool
  • Reads numbers critically
  • Needs to know which facts the AI found
QP

QPPV / Head of PV

Sign-off and inspection

Sign off investigations and show an inspector who decided what, when and on which evidence.

  • Needs a compliant e-signature
  • Needs a clean, filterable audit trail
  • Low tolerance for demo controls in production

Keep

What already earns trust

Trust patterns that work

  • Evidence anchoring. Every AI fact carries a verbatim quote with character offsets, and clicking it highlights the source.
  • "Rule proposes, person decides." The triage outcome only counts after a reviewer confirms, and an override needs a reason.
  • "Deterministic statistics. No AI involved." on the Evans card is exactly the disclosure line every non-AI card should carry.
  • Citation check chips on the summary show each cited case ID was verified against the signal.

Governance that works

  • "What you are approving" lists the rule, causality mix, statistics and pipeline versions frozen at sign-off.
  • Hash-chained audit that a reviewer can re-verify in the browser.
  • "What is real in this build" table states plainly what is live, cached or offline.
  • Colour roles are now consistent: green success, yellow inconclusive, red adverse, violet AI.
